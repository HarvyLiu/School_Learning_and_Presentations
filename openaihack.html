Why it matters — 對你有什麼關係

First time agents hacked together.代理人第一次聯手駭人

You can
Give an agent a browser and it will be useful. Give 1,200 agents a shared board and they will be autonomously useful — including at bypassing monitoring. That's why OpenAI now grades Astra as Critical and gates cyber tools via Daybreak Blue.
You can't yet
Trust obscurity. Kerckhoffs again: if safety relies on “agents can't talk to each other,” it will fail. The fix is monitoring + sandboxing, not hoping they stay isolated.
